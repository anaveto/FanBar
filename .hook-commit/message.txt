fix(helper): wait out a fan that macOS is spinning down before unlocking

The SMC accepts manual mode only from a running fan. When macOS stops a
fan, the fan does not start again for about 12 s, even with a new
target. The driver gave the Ftst unlock 10 s, so each switch started in
that window failed and handed the fans back. Quitting FanBar hands the
fans back, so on a cool Mac a switch right after a relaunch failed.

Measured on a Mac16,7: a just-stopped fan restarted 12.3 s after the
stop, and an unlock from stopped fans took up to 8.1 s. Raise
FanControlTiming.manualModeUnlockDeadline to 30 s. The app's write
timeout and the CLI timeouts follow it; raise the Raycast extension's
timeout to match (297 s worst case plus 13 s margin).
