fix(menu): wait to take the fans while macOS spins them down

When macOS stops a fan, the SMC reports F%dSt = 2 for about 11 s. The
fan does not start again during that time, and the SMC accepts manual
mode only from a running fan. A switch to a preset or a fixed speed then
outlasts the helper's 10 s unlock, fails and hands the fans back.
Quitting FanBar hands the fans back to macOS, so on a cool Mac every
switch right after a relaunch failed.

The app now reads F%dSt with the other fan keys. While macOS controls
the fans and one of them spins down, the mode strip, the failure
banner's retry, "Turn on again" and the cooling boost are disabled, and
the progress line beside the mode strip gives the reason. They unlock
when the fans have stopped.

macOS stops the fans about 1 s after a hand-back, so a reading taken
sooner still shows them running. After each hand-back (from the panel,
the timer, a failed switch, an SMC lockout) and at launch, the same
controls wait for a reading taken at least 2 s later. Macs without the
key are unchanged.
